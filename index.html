    Talking to Tempo

    Write or dictate the way you'd say it. Separate tasks with commas, new lines or "and". Each task can mention:

    2 hours 45 min an hour and a halfHow long it'll take. Leave it out and Tempo guesses from the kind of task and from how long similar tasks took you (shown as ~45m).
    urgent important if I have timePriority (or !high / !low)
    by friday tomorrow by 10/17Due date
    #work or a line like School:Force a category
    I have a chem test fridayBecomes "Study for chem test", due Friday
    pages 40-60 10 problemsSizes the estimate to the amount

    Before you add anything, the list under the box shows what Tempo understood. Click a category, a name or a time to fix it. Things that aren't tasks ("I'm so tired") and duplicates are skipped, but you can add them anyway. When you change a category, Tempo learns from it.

    Study sessions

    A session is one sitting, starting now. Pick tasks from your week or start a fresh list. Set the time it ends (or how long you have, or type "until 6pm"), or leave it blank and Tempo works out when you'll be done, including short breaks. Time you work in a session also counts toward the week.

    How the clock works

    The clock starts at your period's budget and counts down while you run a task timer. Tasks marked done without a timer count their estimate as time spent. Tasks are ranked by priority. Those lower down that don't fit into the time left are labelled won't fit.
