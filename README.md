# Tempo

Tell it what you need to do, in plain words, and it sorts the tasks into categories.
It then checks them against the hours you actually have.

- **Left:** a countdown of the work time left in your period, ticking while you work.
- **Right:** your tasks, grouped by category (School, Work, Home, Errands, Health,
  Money, Personal, or your own).
- Type or dictate something like: *"study for the calc quiz 2 hours by friday, buy
  groceries, gym 1 hour if I have time, urgent: pay the electric bill tomorrow"*.
- Whatever doesn't fit your remaining hours is labelled **won't fit**.
- Timers, focus mode with optional pomodoro, a pace check, and personal
  estimate-accuracy tracking.
- Your data stays in this browser. Use **⋯ → Export backup** now and then.

Live at **https://tempo.caiomsi.com**. To run it locally: `python3 -m http.server` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
Press `?` in the app for shortcuts and the input syntax.
